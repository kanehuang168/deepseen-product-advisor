# Product to creative to performance review

TikTok sellers can start at any relevant stage; the entire chain is optional.

1. Product Advisor supplies facts, hypotheses, sources and unknowns. Existing products skip opportunity discovery.
2. Direction preserves the confirmed script and creates observable requirements. A plan is not footage.
3. Actual filming or authorized production creates a final file. Unavailable tools/budget remain explicit; uncertain paid jobs are queried before another submission.
4. Asset review uses confirmed requirements and real observations. Missing evidence stays unverified; defects return to the responsible step. Playability alone does not pass content.
5. Actual user-controlled advertising creates real reports. Skills do not automatically launch campaigns.
6. Ad analysis separates creative, product/offer, fulfilment and measurement questions, returning only supported actions to the relevant step.

Fictional product, synthetic technical clips and fictional reports demonstrate handoff and arithmetic, not real demand, generated quality or commercial returns. Preserve confirmed scripts, successful outputs, budgets and source versions.
