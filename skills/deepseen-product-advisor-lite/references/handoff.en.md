# Product and workflow handoff

Pass only the facts the next step needs, not full chat, secrets, private customer lists or internal reasoning. Use existing business IDs where available; label synthetic examples rather than pretending they are cloud jobs.

- Product/case, target market, channel, language, audience and objective.
- Supported attributes, source dates, hypotheses and unknowns; unknown capacity or efficacy stays unknown.
- Screening/validation decision, counterevidence and smallest next experiment. Screening does not prove demand.
- Allowed demonstrations, unsupported claims to avoid, exact confirmed script and version where available.
- Actual creative ID/file hash and real review results once a file exists; never prefill acceptance.
- Ad market, account, period, currency, attribution window and click definition. Keep store-wide, organic and ad-attributed revenue separate.

Feedback distinguishes creative, product/offer, fulfilment and measurement problems. Revise only supported changes, preserve originals and successful artifacts, and do not promote one response to a global rule.
